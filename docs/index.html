What it does

A surface that's there without being in the way

A transparent, always-on-top overlay spanning every monitor. It's click-through the rest of the time — use your computer exactly as normal — and only catches your input when you're actively drawing.

Six drawing tools

Freehand pen, eraser, rectangle, ellipse, line, and arrow — each finishes automatically after a stroke so the overlay drops out of the way.

Full color control

A curated palette, a custom color picker, adjustable stroke size, and scroll-wheel cycling to move through colors without opening a menu.

Undo, delete, pan

Per-stroke undo, click-to-delete on individual marks, and a pan mode to drag every drawing into a new position at once.

Persists across reboots

Strokes are written to a per-monitor JSON file. Close the app, restart the machine — your annotations are exactly where you left them.

Hide without losing work

Toggle the whole overlay out of sight and back again — your drawings are never cleared just because you needed a clean screen for a moment.

Lives in the tray

A quiet system tray icon and an info panel with version, author, and license — the app stays out of sight until you press F8.

At your fingertips

Every action has a key

One global hotkey to summon the toolbar from anywhere; the rest work while Vitralis is focused.
